I turn brand briefs into stories people actually stop scrolling for.

Hi, I’m Gia Hao — a Marketing graduate and social-first storyteller with a sharp pulse on digital culture. I bridge creative ideas and real-world execution: planning campaigns, writing copy, shooting on set, and figuring out what makes an audience care.

  1. 01
    Track 01

    The Craft

    From audience insight and scripting to on-site shooting and post-editing, I build native content across Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and Threads. Every frame and caption is crafted to match platform culture while driving real communication goals.

    The Track Record

    Since 2022, I’ve worked across brand communications, 5,000-guest flagship events, and B2B partnerships — from managing 80M+ VND sponsorship budgets to co-directing a nationwide youth campaign that reached over 5,000,000 people.
